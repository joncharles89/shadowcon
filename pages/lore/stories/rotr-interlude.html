Relics of the Realms - Interlude

Chasmreach

“The game has changed,” said the emperor.

Two figures stood by in silence, listening to him, watching him as he strode across his throne room in the highest tower of Chasmreach. The great window behind his throne framed the picturesque vista of the vast city sprawling beneath them, and the state of Caledonia beyond, the principal province of Castelia.

“They're there! Over The Cut, in that wasteland,” he said, pointing randomly at the great window opposite his throne. The ethereal moon Leoth was slowly rising from behind the horizon of Aveinus, the country that lay over the Forbidden Bridge from Caledonia, over the deep chasm known as The Cut.

“Fifty years! For fifty years they have just sat there, ripe for the taking! If only we knew! Then they came and destroyed them, shattered them into pieces and left them like they were nothing!” He finally sat on his throne.

“Are we sure that they are still there?” said the tall lithe figure in almost a whisper.

“Oh, quite sure. Yes, my friend, quite sure,” said the emperor, assuring his right-hand man.

The emperor stood up once more, slowly pacing towards the window overlooking the wasteland of Aveinus.

“What better place to hide such treasures as these? Ulgu—ha, no, far too obvious. The ruinous powers will have armies scouring every shadowy corner of that forsaken realm. No, they came, they found them, shattered them and left them over there in that waste.”

“You're quite sure?” asked the lithe figure.

“My spies seldom fail me, Cardinal, and information paid for confirms it. Sigmar had his toy soldiers dismantle them and leave them over there. In a quiet corner, in the middle of the realm of light, where nothing, not even truth, can be hidden. Pah!”

“Then we send an army to scour—”

“No!” The emperor interrupted the cardinal. “It will leave us open. It will leave us weak. We have pirates to the south, bonereapers to the east, and only the moons know what the north has in wait for us. No, we need to find...”

“Others,” continued a shorter man, concealed in the only shadow in the room. The Cardinal had almost forgotten he was still there.

“We need to send others. Tell them they are hunting treasure, give it to them, give them the land, free of tax and caveats. Then offer riches for these pieces. Call them what they are, the Shards of Sigmar, they're—”

“No! It draws too much attention. Call them...” He stopped to think for a moment, running his fingers over the sharp point on his axe. “Call them Relics of the Realms,” he finished as he sat on his throne.

“Alert the eight principalities of Castelia. Inform them that under my decree they have licence to pull together four of their finest treasure hunters with a small retinue. They can keep whatever treasure they find, and claim whatever land they win, but these relics will be returned to the palace. Failure to do so will result in death.”

For a split second, the glint in the emperor's eye shone the yellow of Aethergold.

“It will be done,” said the shadowy figure, silently retreating from the room.

“At last, my friend. They will be ours.”
