Two Years Passed

The tales were told in taverns, temples, brothels, and on battlefields.

We all know of the relics of a time gone past. Those weapons bestowed upon the mightiest of heroes; those guardians chosen to stave off the tide of ruination, where the veil grows thin between the Mortal Realms and that Darkness. The Realm of Nightmare.

We all heard tell of how, all those years ago, through misfortune, calamity, or fate, of how those Shards came, by hook or by crook, to the silver deserts of Aveinus. That wasteland beyond the Cut; split from Castelia by roaring white rapids.

Then how those obsidian warriors, of that mysterious Chamber Ignotus, came down from Azyr to break them, to shatter them, and to spread them across the desert, hiding them in the realm where most would not think to look. The realm of illumination and truth. Hysh.

We were there when the emperor of Castelia opened the gates to the cursed deserts of Aveinus, for hunters, thieves, grave robbers and opportunists. There, land was grabbed, treasure found and those mighty Shards of Sigmar, reforged, remade and brought before the emperor.

But the emperor is no fool. He knows the glory of the Shards is tarnished by betrayal. He hides them in plain sight across the regal empire of Castelia, and that fledgling country born of conquest and claim. Aveinus.

In nine towers sit these weapons of great power. Nine, for one of the eight Shards, the twin daggers, Umbra and Sombra had been sundered into two, one the essence of Ulgu; falsehoods and shadows, the other corrupted by ruination, lust for war and the ever-changing appetites of plague.

Their very existence is an insult to the powers of Azyr.

A threat.

Nay a promise.

But a new threat emerges. One the emperor did not foresee. An uprising in the north from Aveinus.

For two years, barons have ruled over this wasteland in the emperor's name, promising riches and wealth to those who followed. Instead, they have been ravaged by plague, famine and drought. Tribal raids and ground-shaking quakes. Sandstorms that go on for days, blinding the meek and strangling the brave.

The pleas of the despairing barons fell on the deaf ears of the emperor, for his gaze lay elsewhere.

His enemies gather as the day grows late for Vaelon IV, Emperor of Castelia and Aveinus, for the time is almost at hand for his final act.

His endgame.
