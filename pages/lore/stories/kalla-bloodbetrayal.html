Blood & Betrayal (1832-3)

Not long after the events of Relics of the Realms.

At the summit of the grand Calat'on Tower, the throne room crowned Caledonia, the diadem of Castelia. From its lofty height, one could see Shimmer-sea Bay, the mountains of Moonsreach and, on a clear day, the silver expanse of Aveinus; that once-cursed desert, now part of the Imperial Frontier under the rule of Emperor Vaelon IV.

The throne room was magnificent in all its splendour. A dawn-metal throne stood at the centre of a marble sea, its dark surface softly reflecting the light of the realm beyond. Heavy, beautiful tapestries hung from the walls, each depicting a triumph in the long history of the empire.

The messenger's glassy eyes remained fixed upon one depicting the defeat of Ulubor, the great Orruk warlord, fought in a faraway land she would never see. Her body lay still upon the marble, her neck broken.

“Why do they test me? Why?” The emperor uttered, his voice exhausted.

The cardinal stood at the side of the throne, remaining as still as he was silent.

The emperor was pacing the throne room, the setting red sun outside casting his long shadow across the marble.

His rage had finally calmed.

“They have the wealth they took from the land, they have their own wealth, what else can I give them. I'm no farmer, or doctor. I can't stop these disasters, yet they stand outside and bang on my door day and night and… no. It stops here. Tell them no, they need to look to their own failings; reach into their own pockets. Send an envoy to each Baron, I want their affairs in order, I want the populace under control otherwise, there will be bloody retribution.”

“Right away”, the cardinal slunk out of the throne room down the spiral staircase, sensing the emperor's ire kindling once more.

Down through the tower the cardinal made his way with haste, until he turned a corner out from the stair hall to be confronted by a small man with greying hair.

“Oh, sorry sir I didn't mean…”

It was one of valets, he was pale, sweaty and in a rush.

“What is it?” Asked the Cardinal, sensing something was amiss.

“It's… it…it's.” He was deep in panic.

“Spit it out!” The Cardinal had no time for this.

“It's Kalla sir, the Sword, the Shard of Azyr”

“What about it?”

“Well, it's gone sir”

“What do you mean it's gone”

“It's gone, sir; some of the guards are dead, the sigils are broken and the sword is missing, it's been taken”

“Who? Who has the…”

“I don't want to say sir, but there are guards who saw the Judiciar going into the tower, but the Judiciar hadn't been seen since. They bel…”

“Not here, too many ears.”

The Cardinal lead him into the privacy of stair hall, keeping his arm around the greying panicked man.

“Tell his greatness, he will need to know immediately. And tell no one else. Do you understand? No one.”

“But… but”

“No time to waste!” The Cardinal rushed down the staircase, down into the bowels of the tower. He made his way to the door that led to the courtyard. He needed to pen letters and send envoys before moonrise.

Just as he opened it a body hit the ground in front of him, breaking on the cobblestone.

The Cardinal looked down at the valet. The visage of fear on his face etched as if in stone.

A pool of blood began to spread.

The Cardinal stepped over him as if he were a drunk in the streets.

No time he thought.

A servant passed him on his way to his study.

“You, walk with me”

“M'lord” The servant swiftly moved into step with the Cardinal.

“There is a body in the courtyard. Remove it. Down into the cut with it. Not a word.”

“Yes… Yes m'lord” the servant stuttered slightly. The Cardinal knew he would have to cut this one's throat before the end of the night.

“Then I need you to bring me the captain of the watch for the eastern tower and battlements.”

“Yes m'lord”

“Then wine to my study, quick boy! Go!”

The servant scurried off with impressive speed.

*

The servant had come with his wine, then took a nasty fall out of the Cardinal's window which overlooked the great chasm that sat between Castelia and Aveinus. White rapids cascaded down from the Sea of Mirrors to Shimmer-sea Bay with a constant roar.

He had almost finished writing his letters to the barons when a restrained, yet heavy knock rolled out a tattoo on his door.

“Come” said the Cardinal.

The captain of the watch opened the door and stepped in.

“Captain, please come in, take a seat. Wine?”

“No thank you sir. On duty sir” said the stiff man as he sat opposite the Cardinal. He was a weathered man, but strong. His frame was impressive.

“Quite right, my apologies captain”

“I was informed that you requested my presence sir”

“Yes, I won't take much of your time. There has been an incident, or so I hear”

“Sir, there was. A valet was sent to inform the emperor in person before all else. My apologies sir, I should have sent word to you as well”

“That's quite all right, Captain… Grimes, is it?”

“Vimes sir”

“Vimes, I do apologise. Captain, what preliminary findings has your investigation brought to light?”

“We believe that the suspect entered the eastern tower with ease. I believe the guards may have known the suspect sir, as they were killed from behind. The suspect walked past them into the tower and then struck, minimising the risk of being seen sir.”

“How were they struck captain?”

"Mayweather was stabbed at the base of the skull, whilst Travis was burned from the inside out; the only mark left on his body was at the base of the skull. No witnesses as of yet sir."

“Terrible captain, extend our deepest sympathies to their families. Carry on, what else have you found”

“Much of the same sir. The suspect made their way up the tower, executing all from behind in much the same way. Silently. Eight dead in total sir.”

The Cardinal tutted and stood from behind his desk and sat himself next to the captain and poured himself another drink.

“Are you sure I can't tempt you Captain Vimes”

“Thank you, sir, but no”

“Carry on Captain”

“The sigil seems to have been shattered by something that would leave it cracked and burned, the marble around it seems to have melted and bubbled up at high heat then set again, as if the heat that shattered it was intense but fast.”

“I see, carry-on”

“The casket that held the sword was shattered in much the same way, what was left of the Dawnwood was charred.”

“And the assailant left with the sword, unseen as he had murdered the guards. I see. Do we have any idea if he has left the capital yet?”

“Well, that sir, ties a lot together”

“How so captain?”

“Well, the suspect didn't come down the tower again, we believe, sir. We think he left from the turret roof sir. There is one final mark, again, fast and intense heat has cracked the brick work and left scorch marks. And there is one more thing sir, this symbol seems to have been made by the most burned parts sir”

The captain handed the Cardinal a scrap of vellum with a symbol, a two-sided point with a circle at the tip. the Cardinal knew it immediately: the symbol for Azyr, the Celestial Guardians, the Stormcast Eternals.

“Is there anything else captain?”

“One more thing sir, but I don't like to say…”

“You may confide in me captain”

“There are some of the guards who believed they saw the emperor's Judiciar enter the tower not too long before Mayweather and Travis were found. I know I'm talking out of turn sir, and they were probably drunk and will be flogged sir, but I thought you'd best have all of the fact's sir”

“Captain, captain, captain, you have done a sterling job thus far.” The Cardinal stood and led the captain to the door.

“Carry on with your investigation and come back to me when you have more. Oh, and when you have time captain, I would like to speak with all on duty, perhaps at the end of the watch? Would you gather everyone into the garrison for me please.”

“Sir”

And with that the Captain strode off.

*

The throne room was broken. All four windows shattered. The tapestries were gone with the wind that blew through, the marble floor was cracked all the way through. The emperor was sat upon what was left of his throne with bloody hands and wild eyes that bore into the Cardinal's very soul.

“You've heard?” He growled.

“I have sire. I have silenced the guard and had them replaced. A notice has gone around about the treasury being broken into. I thought with the rebellion in the north, it may sway some sympathy if they knew we had our own problems. Maybe buy us some time.”

“It shows weakness.” The emperor stood tall.

The cardinal felt his blood chill.

“Have you sent word to the Barons?”

“I have my lord. I will also be sending a garrison to accompany sheriffs to each settlement to ensure everything is in order.”

“Good”

“The other problem we have is…”

“My sword has been taken my dear Cardinal. Have you letters or words that can bring it back. You know the importance of these weapons, these Shards” spat the emperor, as he walked forward. The Cardinal stepped back.

“Yes sire, I am aware.” His voice held a slight tone of fear.

“Then what are you doing?”

“Sire, I fear we may need to raise a fyrd, we need numbers to search out our old ally.”

The emperor stopped.

“So, it is true. He has betrayed us.” He turned away from the Cardinal, considering the betrayal of the Judiciar, the Justice of the Realm.

The emperor's voice was little more than a whisper. His face was contorted with frustration and disbelief.

“It's more than that.”

The Cardinal handed the emperor the vellum he had taken from the captain and repeated what he had been told.
