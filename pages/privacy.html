ShadowCon ("we", "our", or "us") operates www.shadowcon.co.uk as a fan-run tabletop event platform. We are committed to safeguarding your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What We Collect and Why

  • Interactive Features & Map Usage: When you use the site’s interactive tools, schedules, and event maps, basic technical data (such as session states, IP addresses, and browser details) is processed to keep the site functioning and secure (Lawful basis: Legitimate interests).
  • Registration & Attendee Records: If you register, join an event roster, or submit details through the site, we collect your name, email, and player/team details to organise matchups, manage capacity, and provide event communications (Lawful basis: Contract performance).
  • Contact & Enquiries: Any information submitted via email or contact forms is processed solely to respond to your queries (Lawful basis: Legitimate interests).

Cookies and Storage

The site may use essential local storage or cookies solely to maintain your session (e.g. navigation preferences or map states). We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking scripts.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Limited data may be processed by:

  • Hosting & Infrastructure Providers: To deliver the website, maps, and ensure uptime and security.
  • Event Coordinators: Organisers accessing attendee lists solely for pairing, scheduling, and running event logistics.

Photography and Event Media

Casual photography and videos may occur during the physical event. If you do not wish to be included in published community photos, simply inform the event organisers on the day.

Your Rights

  • Your Rights: Under UK data protection law, you have the right to request access to your personal data, rectify inaccuracies, or request its deletion.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ashadowinthewest@gmail.com. You may also contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you have unresolved data protection concerns.
