Event Details

ShadowCon 2027 - Kalla Reborn is a narrative wargaming event for Warhammer: Age of Sigmar. Each player will require a 1,250 point army plus an additional character as below.

This year you will be a member of one of 3 teams representing either the Emperor Vaelon, the rebel forces or the strike team of Kalla herself. Each team will have their own objectives to complete over the weekend

Army building

In order to ensure balanced gameplay, whilst encouraging players to bring fun and thematic armies, the following army building rules are in place:

  • Maximum army points: 1,250
  • Your army must be split into 3 sections; FORERUNNERS, WARHOST and GUARDIANS.
  • Each section must have at least 1 unit within it, normal army building regiment rules do not apply.
    • If a unit warscroll contains rules that refer to regiment, treat those rules as applying to the section instead.
  • Your army must also include an additional character depending on your team:
    • Emperor Vaelon: Imperial Emissary
    • Rebel Forces - Rebel Commander
    • Kalla's Strike Team - Ignotus Hunter
    This unit has no points value and cannot be assigned Artifacts of Power or Heroic Traits. They do however gain the keyword of your army (SERAPHON, SKAVEN etc).
    Please aim for nothing larger than a 40mm base (contact us if unsure). Anybody who is attempting to be cheesy will be punished! We don't expect everyone to go out of their way to build a custom model but there may be an award...
  • If you wish to use a custom battletome, please contact the NEOs.

Sportsmanship

This one is definitely a "we shouldn't have to say it but we're going to anyway", you are expected to play in the spirit of the game and the event.

We only have one rule when it comes to life: Don't be a dick. So that's the rule for ShadowCon. Don't be aggressive, argumentative or confrontational with your co-players, the NEOs or the venue staff. If you're being a REAL dick, we'll just ask you to leave.

Model requirements

We understand that not everyone enjoys painting their toy soldiers, but we do ask that your army be finished to at least a basic tabletop standard. That means fully painted and based.

Just so it's clear: you will not be allowed to use any unfinished or unpainted models in this event. If you try to, we'll take them off the table and let the 40k players have them.

Models should be on the correct size base where possible (see the AOS app) but honestly as long as you're not doing it to be a beardy nerd, any round/oval base should be fine as long as it's for stability/aesthetic reasons.

Date, Location & Facilities

Saturday 23rd - Sunday 24th October 2027
Tettenhall College, Wolverhampton, WV6 8QX

  • There's a large car park with plenty of space for all.
  • Lunch will be provided on both Saturday and Sunday.
  • Men's, women's and accessible toilet facilities are available on site
  • There will be refreshments (including alcohol) available.
  • You have the option of adding accommodation on to your event ticket. This is in the boarding house on site for Friday and Saturday night and includes breakfast on Saturday and Sunday morning.
